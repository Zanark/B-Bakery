## Step inside

The padded bakery doorway waits for a click, tap or keyboard activation.
The doors open, the hanging bell moves and a short local bell sound is attempted
from that gesture. A visible **Skip entrance** link always offers a direct route in.

The website keeps a simple order: **Home, Our little story, Cakes we've made,
How to order**. Navigation stays visible on mobile.

## Category notebooks

The introduction keeps its original cream background in a full-width section, without a notebook
frame or spiral. Its cake, headline and two actions remain; Our little story
still follows as its own section.

**Cakes we've made** is grouped into six top-spiral notebooks:
**Birthday Cakes, Tea cakes, Cream cakes, Decorated cakes, Doll cakes and Chocolates**.
Each cover has its own category-themed sticker pair rather than repeated hearts
and bows.
The notepads use clean paper faces, fine sheet edges and paired top-wire rings,
without a wide left-hand spine.
Click a category to zoom into its notebook. The image-free first page offers
**1/2 KG, 1 KG, 1.5 KG, 2 KG, 2.5 KG and 3 KG** as preferred weights; size and
availability are confirmed personally.

Subsequent pages hold **four cakes per page**, with fewer on the final page when
necessary. Use **Previous page / Next page**, the arrow keys, or drag the bottom
edge upward around the top binding. **All categories** returns to the covers.
Every cake retains **View larger photo** and **Ask about this cake**.

The twelve previously reviewed examples currently fill the Cream cakes,
Decorated cakes and Doll cakes notebooks.
The owner-confirmed Black Forest cake is on the Cream cover. Selected Birthday,
Tea and Chocolate sources still need prepared/reviewed cutouts; no substitute
product images are invented.

readable body text are retained. Ruled lines stay away from reading text.
The notebooks support click, touch, Enter and Space, with visible keyboard focus.
Reduced motion, forced colours, missing enhancements and no JavaScript show the
complete category pages directly. Automated browser checks are not final visual
or native-device approval.

## Repository and publication

The display name is **Whisk & Crumbs**. The repository is still
[Zanark/WhisksAndCrumbs](https://github.com/Zanark/WhisksAndCrumbs), and the existing
website URL remains unchanged until the owner renames the repository.

GitHub Pages publishes only the **125 generated files in `site/`**, including the
dedicated category-notebook module.
This README uses the same public-ready images. Authoring sources, original photos,
the account export, masks, private reviews and detailed maintenance guides remain
local; the public snapshot is not their backup.

The display-name choice is approved. Brand availability, trademark rights and
legal uniqueness have not been established.
